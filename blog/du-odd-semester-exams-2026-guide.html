DU Odd Semester Exams 2026: Dates, Date Sheet & 60-Day PYQ Plan

If you've just joined Delhi University, your timetable probably looks like alphabet soup: DSC-1, GE-2, SEC-3, VAC-1, AEC. Here's what each of those actually means.

← All Guides

By Sulaksh Editorial · Reviewed by Sulaksh Editorial Team · Updated 25 September 2026

Delhi University has confirmed the broad schedule for the 2026–27 odd semester. If you are in Semester 1, 3, 5 or 7, here is everything that matters — and how to use the next 60 days.

Key dates (official academic calendar)

EventDate
Classes beganJuly 28, 2026

The detailed paper-wise date sheet is released separately on DU's examination portal (exam.du.ac.in) — usually 3–4 weeks before exams begin. Track it on our DU tentative datesheet page too. Check venues and shifts the day it drops; they change every semester.

Why the next 60 days decide your SGPA

DU's UGCF papers are strictly unit-wise, and question themes repeat heavily. Analysis of recent papers shows roughly 40% of question themes recur every 2–3 years. That makes previous-year papers the single highest-ROI study material you own.

The 60-day plan

Now → Nov 19 (regular classes): syllabus mapping + PYQ-first study. For each paper: download the syllabus, mark every unit, then solve the last 5 years of PYQs before deep theory revision. Build a topic-frequency table — the 6–8 topics carrying ~70% of marks become obvious. The 3-pass PYQ method walks through exactly how.

Nov 20 → Dec 3 (prep leave): timed papers. One full 3-hour handwritten paper per subject per week. DU rewards presentation: structured answers with flowcharts and diagrams score disproportionately well.
