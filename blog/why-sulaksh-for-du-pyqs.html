Why Students Use Sulaksh for DU PYQs

I built my Sem 1 prep around scattered PDFs and lost a week just organising them. Here is what Sulaksh does differently — features only, no hype.

← All Guides

By Sulaksh Editorial · Reviewed by Sulaksh Editorial Team · Updated 1 October 2026 · 7 min read

Hey all! Quick honesty first: no website tops your syllabus or replaces solving papers yourself. What a good archive does is remove friction — find the right paper in seconds, see syllabus + notes next to it, open it on your phone without logins. That is the whole job Sulaksh tries to do. Below is what exists today, what it costs (nothing), and how to use it without wasting your prep week like I did.

What Sulaksh actually provides

  • Large paper archive in one search. The PYQ archive search looks across 23,000+ stored papers by paper name. Type "corporate accounting" or "data privacy" and open any match directly — no login, no paywall.
  • Semester-wise hubs, not dumps. Each subject hub (for example B.Com Hons, Political Science, English) is split Sem 1–8 with syllabus, PYQs and notes in taught order. You see sequence, not a random folder.
  • Syllabus + PYQ + notes together. The DU & College section keeps the official syllabus next to past papers, so you can map which unit each question came from — the core of the 9+ CGPA method.
  • Free view and save. Every document opens in the viewer and can be saved for offline revision. No sign-up for viewing.
  • Study guides around the papers. The guides explain systems students actually trip on — attendance math, 25-mark internals, GE choice — so papers make sense in context.

How to use it in 20 minutes

  1. Open your hub. From DU & College, pick programme → subject → semester.
  2. Download syllabus first. Copy unit titles in order — one page per unit in your notebook.
  3. Search three past papers. In the archive search, type each subject name, open the last three years, mark which unit each question belongs to.
  4. Revise by units, not pages. Repeated units get double time. That single habit lifted my internals more than any all-nighter.
Honest limits: Sulaksh is student-run, not a DU official site. Always verify final paper code and semester against your college handbook and DU Exam Portal. If a PDF looks mismatched, report via Contact — corrections go live after verification.

New to DU? Read North truth, South truth, off-campus truth and the comparison before picking societies and PGs. Papers are identical everywhere — daily life is what differs.

Frequently asked questions (answered by the author)

Q1: Is Sulaksh free?
Yes — viewing is free with no sign-up. Saving works from the viewer for offline study.

Q2: Is this official DU material?
Question papers and syllabi come from DU's published examinations and UGCF/NEP documents. Sulaksh itself is independent and student-run — not a University of Delhi website. Verify codes with your college.

Q3: How many papers are there?
The archive search covers 23,000+ stored papers across courses and years. Semester hubs surface the most-used ones first so you don't drown.

Q4: Can I request a missing paper?
Yes — via Contact with subject + semester + year. Uploads appear after verification, not instantly.

Q5: Does it work on phone?
Yes — viewer and search are mobile-first. Save key PDFs before metro rides; network dies exactly when you need that one unit.

Last-minute advice from the author

Don't collect 200 PDFs. Collect syllabus + three PYQs per subject, map units, and start writing answers. That is the entire system. Everything else on Sulaksh is just organised around that habit.

Stuck finding a paper? Message via Contact with the exact paper name — I will point you to the hub. Now close this tab and solve one paper today.
