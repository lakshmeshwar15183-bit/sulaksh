Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sulaksh ("we", "our" or "us"), an educational platform operated by Lakshmeshwar Pandey and Suyash Shukla, collects, uses, stores and protects the information of visitors and users of our website.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways when you visit or use Sulaksh:

  • Information you provide voluntarily: for example, when you contact us, we may receive your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Usage data (automatically collected): when you browse the website, we may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent and referring pages.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: we and our advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to recognise your browser, remember your preferences and understand how the website is used.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain and improve the website and our educational content;
  • Respond to your questions, requests and feedback;
  • Understand how visitors use our website to improve performance and usability;
  • Display advertising relevant to you, where applicable;
  • Detect, prevent and address technical issues or security concerns; and
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. We use cookies to remember your preferences (such as your selected theme), to understand usage patterns and to support advertising and analytics.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect some features of the website. For more information about cookies, you may visit your browser's help section.

4. Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors interact with the website. These services collect information such as pages visited, time on site and approximate location, which helps us improve our content. You can learn more about how Google handles data at Google's privacy page and can opt out where available.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

Sulaksh may display advertisements and may work with advertising partners such as Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to our site or other websites.

  • Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on your browsing activity.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • You may learn more about how Google uses data when you use partner sites by visiting Google's "How Google uses data" page.
  • You can also opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

We do not use personal or sensitive information to serve advertisements without appropriate consent where required by law.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to operate the website, including hosting, analytics, advertising and email services. These providers may have access to certain non-personal or operational information solely to perform services on our behalf and are required to keep it confidential.

The website may also contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external site you visit.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website provides educational content intended for learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without appropriate consent from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove that information.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, update or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests in a timely manner.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
