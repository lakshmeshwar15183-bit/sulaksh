👋 About Us

Who We Are

Sulaksh is a free, student-first study platform built by students, for students. We organise notes, previous year question papers, syllabi and question banks so that you spend less time searching and more time studying.

🎯 Our Story & Mission

Sulaksh started with a simple observation: students preparing for Delhi University courses and one-day government exams waste countless hours hunting for reliable study material across Telegram groups, random Drive folders and dead links. The material usually exists — it is just scattered, unorganised and hard to trust.

Our mission is to fix that. Every subject on Sulaksh is organised into clean folders — Syllabus, Notes, PYQs and Question Banks — semester by semester, exam by exam. Whether you are looking for a DU SEC, VAC, AEC or GE paper, a B.A. or B.Com Programme syllabus, or last year's question paper for a competitive exam, everything sits exactly where you expect it to be.

We keep the platform completely free. No paywalls, no sign-up walls for study content. Sulaksh is run by students who have been through the same struggle, funded by nothing more than passion (and maybe a few ads someday to cover server costs).

📚 What We Offer

  • Du & College section — SEC, VAC, AEC and GE course materials plus Core Subjects with semester-wise syllabus, notes and PYQs.
  • One-Day Exams section — curated material for SSC, banking, railways and other government exams.
  • Quick tools — dedicated pages for notes, PYQs, syllabus and important questions.

🤝 Why Students Trust Us

  • Clean, predictable structure — no endless scrolling through chats.
  • Quality-checked: all materials are thoroughly verified by Lakshmeshwar Pandey before they go live.
  • Community-driven: anyone can report wrong or suspicious content instantly via our contact page.
  • We respect creators — if any material infringes your rights, we remove it promptly.
  • Built and maintained by students who actually use it.

👨‍💻 About the Author — Lakshmeshwar Pandey

Hi, I’m Lakshmeshwar Pandey, a Delhi University student and the creator of Sulaksh. I built Sulaksh in 2025 because I kept seeing the same problem in my own college — syllabus scattered across PDFs, PYQs lost in WhatsApp, and juniors asking “bhai, notes kahan se milega?” every semester. I wanted a single, clean place where a first-year and a final-year could both find exactly what they need in two taps.

Every folder on Sulaksh is checked by me or a senior before it goes live. If a PDF is not yet uploaded, you’ll see a short, verified overview that says clearly — please verify from your college for your batch; this will vanish the moment the official file is uploaded. No copied content, no clickbait.
