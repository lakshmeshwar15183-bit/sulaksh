GE: Indian Constitution — Is It the Right GE for You?

GE Constitution is the most chosen GE at DU — 4 credits, high overlap with PolSci, and the easiest to score if you pick it for the right reason.

← All Guides

“Introduction to the Indian Constitution” is a 4-credit GE offered to almost every DU course under UGCF. Because it overlaps with Class 12 Political Science and UPSC basics, students think “easy marks”. It is — but only if you study it like a law paper, not a history chapter.

What’s actually in the syllabus

Four units: 1) Making of the Constitution, 2) Fundamental Rights & Duties, 3) Organs of the State (Legislature/Executive/Judiciary), 4) Federalism & Local Government. Unit 2 is the heaviest — Articles 12-35, with one case example per Right, gets a 15-mark question every year.

Who should pick it

  • Pick it if: You’re from History/Political Science/Sociology and can handle bare acts. Overlap saves you 30% effort.
  • Avoid if: You want pure theory with no articles. This paper rewards those who can quote Article numbers correctly; wrong numbers lose marks faster than blank answers.

How toppers make notes here

One page per Unit: left side article number → what it says in one line, right side one Supreme Court case or amendment. Example: Art 21 — “Right to life” → Maneka Gandhi (1978) expands it to dignity. That format is exactly how examiners check.

Scoring trick: In the exam, write “Art. 32 — Right to Constitutional Remedies — heart and soul of the Constitution (Ambedkar)” — that single line signals you know both article and context, worth 2 extra marks per answer.

PYQs for this GE are in GE → Indian Constitution → PYQs on Sulaksh. Solve last 3 years — 50% repeat is Article 32, 44, 356. Verify paper code (GE-1 vs GE-2) from your college timetable; content is 95% same.

FAQ

Is this based on the latest DU pattern? Yes — UGCF/NEP 2022 as taught in 2025-26. Verify paper codes from your college handout.
