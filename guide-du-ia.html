DU Internal Assessment — Don’t Leave 25 Marks on the Table

IA is 25 marks per paper — presentation, assignment, attendance — and the cheapest SGPA boost DU offers if you know the breakup.

← All Guides

Every DSC/GE paper at DU (UGCF) is 75 marks end-sem + 25 marks Internal Assessment (IA). IA is not “grace marks” — it is presentation (10) + assignment/project (10) + attendance (5) in most colleges, and the marking gap between a 6 SGPA and a 7 SGPA is often just 15 IA marks across 5 papers.

The breakup — college by college

  • Presentation: 10 marks for a 5-7 minute class presentation. Slides are 40% of marks — a neat 6-slide deck with one PYQ reference beats a 20-slide clutter.
  • Assignment: 10 marks for one handwritten or typed submission. DU checks Unit-wise coverage — an assignment that cites the syllabus Unit number gets full.
  • Attendance: 5 marks, slabbed (e.g., >85% =5, 80-85%=4). One bunk less per fortnight is a full mark here.

How to lock these without stress

  1. Week 2: Ask your teacher for the assignment topic and IA rubric — don’t guess.
  2. Make the assignment in the same one-page-per-unit format as your notes; you’ll reuse it for revision.
  3. For presentation, speak 5 minutes with one quote/diagram from the syllabus; reading slides aloud is where students lose 4 marks.
Math: 25 marks × 7 papers = 175 marks per semester that don’t need exam pressure. A student with 20/25 average IA needs only 40/75 in theory for a 60 — versus 65/75 without IA.

Check your college’s IA notice for exact splits; Sulaksh keeps the relevant syllabus Units alongside PYQs so your IA topic and exam revision overlap.
