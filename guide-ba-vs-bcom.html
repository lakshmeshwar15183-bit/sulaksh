BA Programme vs B.Com Programme

An honest, brochure-free comparison of workload, syllabus style and career direction for DU students choosing between the two programme degrees.

← All Guides

Every admission season, thousands of DU students stand at the same fork: BA Programme or B.Com Programme. Relatives have opinions, friends have joined whatever their friends joined, and college brochures describe both as "holistic learning experiences." Here is a plainer comparison.

The structural difference

BA Programme typically pairs disciplines from humanities and social sciences — combinations like Political Science with History, Hindi with Philosophy, or a language with Psychology, depending on your college. You learn to read widely, argue from evidence and write long-form answers.

B.Com Programme anchors you to the commerce family: financial accounting, business law, corporate taxation basics, cost accounting and economics. The skill stack is more technical and rule-based — formats, procedures, computations.

Programme courses (vs Honours) mean you study two disciplines at moderate depth instead of one at full depth. The trade-off is breadth over specialisation.

Workload reality

  • B.Com Programme: regular problem-solving practice. Accounting papers punish last-night cramming because speed comes from repetition. Expect steady weekly work.
  • BA Programme: heavy reading and writing cycles around submissions and exams. Flexible week-to-week, but answer-writing stamina decides your marks.

Where each one points

  • Headed toward CA/CS/CMA foundation, banking or accounting jobs? B.Com keeps your base warm — the syllabus overlaps directly with what those exams test.
  • Thinking of civil services, law, teaching, journalism, psychology or public policy? BA's essay-based subjects build exactly the analytical writing that UPSC mains and law entrance demand.
  • Planning an MBA? Both work; B.Com helps for finance specialisations, BA for people-oriented roles.
Honest rule of thumb: choose by the subject you can study on your worst day, not the career you imagine on your best day. Three years is long when you hate your papers.

What stays common

Both programmes carry the same SEC, VAC, AEC and GE structure under NEP, so your generic-skills exposure (communication, environmental science, digital tools) is comparable. Your electives and internships matter more for differentiation than the programme label itself.

A practical next step

Before deciding, open the actual syllabus of both programmes and skim all six semesters — not the prospectus summary, the real unit-wise syllabus. You will feel within minutes which set of units you would rather live with for three years. Both programmes' semester-wise syllabi are available in the 📦 BA / B.Com Programme section on Sulaksh if you want to compare them side by side.

Neither degree is "easier" universally. One matches how your brain likes to work; pick that one.

FAQ

Is B.Com Programme easier than BA Programme? Neither is objectively easier. B.Com demands regular numerical practice; BA demands sustained reading and long-answer writing. Ease depends on which skill comes more naturally to you.

Can I switch from Programme to Honours later? Some DU colleges allow upgrades based on first-year performance, but rules vary by college and seats are limited. Check your specific college's policy before counting on it.

Which programme scores better CGPA? Scoring depends on your aptitude match, not the degree name. Students generally score higher in subjects requiring the kind of work they will actually do daily.
