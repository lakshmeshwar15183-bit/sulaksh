About Political Science Major — All Semesters PYQ — Delhi University

Political Science Major at DU for All Semesters blends theory and Indian institutions. This All Semesters PYQ with 83 document(s) orders concept and application together. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Theory — liberty, justice, democracy, Rawls/Nozick
  • Unit 2: Indian constitution — federalism, rights, institutions
  • Unit 3: Comparative & global politics — case studies
  • Unit 4: Long answers — thesis + counter-thesis + Indian example

Exam pattern & marking: 90/100 marks with choices “Answer 4 out of 6”. Theory asks comparison, India papers ask application with recent examples. Thesis + counter-thesis + one Supreme Court case or election data fetch full marks.
