About English Honours — Semester 1 — Delhi University

English Honours at DU (NEP/UGCF) for Semester 1 is built around close reading of primary texts. This page Semester 1 with 20 document(s) organises semester 1 material in DU's taught order. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Primary texts — e.g., Chaucer, Donne, Shakespeare (form-aware reading)
  • Unit 2: Literary history & close reading — passage-based analysis
  • Unit 3: Theory — e.g., Post-colonial, Feminist, Cultural studies
  • Unit 4: Essay & long answers — thesis + textual reference

Exam pattern & marking: Most English papers are 90 theory + 10 internal or 75+25. Questions are 10 marks (short) and 15 marks (long), with passage-based questions where 1-2 quoted lines matter. Examiners reward accurate textual reference, a clear thesis in first 3 lines, and awareness of form. One-page-per-text sheet with “what's said / how it's said / one quote” maps to rubric.

How to prepare & what is missing: Open syllabus Units 1-4 alongside the 3 most recent PYQs and mark which Unit each question came from.
