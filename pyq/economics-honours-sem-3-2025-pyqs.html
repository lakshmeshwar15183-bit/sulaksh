About Economics Honours — Semester 3 2025 — Delhi University

Economics Honours at DU for Semester 3 is mathematical and diagram-driven. This Semester 3 2025 with 12 document(s) separates quantitative core first. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Micro/Macro foundations — consumer equilibrium, IS-LM
  • Unit 2: Numericals — elasticity, national income, multiplier
