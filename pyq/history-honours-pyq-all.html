About History Honours — All Semesters PYQ — Delhi University

History Honours at DU for All Semesters spans Ancient to Contemporary. This All Semesters PYQ page with 37 document(s) groups syllabus and PYQs chronologically.

Units (verify from your college syllabus handout):

  • Unit 1: Sources & historiography — epigraphy, archaeology, literary
  • Unit 2: Polity & economy — e.g., Mauryan state, Sultanate, 1857
  • Unit 3: Society & culture — Bhakti, gender, caste
  • Unit 4: Map & long essays — locate 4 sites + historiographical debate
