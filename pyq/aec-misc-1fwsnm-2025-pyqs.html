About सोशल मीडिया और ब्लॉग लेखन — Delhi University

सोशल मीडिया और ब्लॉग लेखन (Hindi Ga) is a 2-credit AEC (2-0-0) for students who studied Hindi till Class 8th, taught in 2025-26 as AEC-3 (p.81 credit table, du.ac.in/uploads/new-web/24022023_AEC.pdf). As per the official syllabus at that live PDF, the course has इकाई 1 सोशल मीडिया और ब्लॉग (social media arth/paribhasha, prabhav/mahatva, types: Wikipedia/blog/social networking/Twitter/YouTube/Instagram, and blog lekhan) and इकाई 2 सोशल मीडिया का व्यावहारिक पक्ष — including preparing a vigyapan for a samajik abhiyan, creating one's own blog, reporting on a social-media news item and writing an article on social media topics, with readings like Ravindra Prabhat and Swarna Suman (pp.82-83). Assessment is कुल अंक 50: लिखित 38, आंतरिक 12 (p.83). Students lose marks by writing theory about platforms without preparing the vigyapan/blog/report samples the practical asks, which the 38-mark paper checks (as of 12 Sept 2026).

Official sources: University of Delhi · DU Exam Portal · your college's सोशल मीडिया और ब्लॉग लेखन handout. Verify paper codes and units for your batch.

FAQs

How is this PYQ marked at DU?

Typically 75 marks written + 25 internal or 90+10 depending on college. Shorts are 10 marks, longs are 15. Steps, working notes and one labelled diagram or correct quote/data point per answer usually cross 7/10.

Which units repeat most in this subject?

Middle units (2-3) — theory plus application — repeat about 50% of the time in 2023-2025 PYQs. Map each past question to Units 1-4; the 10-minute PYQ-mapping exercise tells you where to spend the next two days.

Is this the latest UGCF/NEP pattern?

Yes — papers from 2023-2026 follow the current UGCF/NEP framework. Pre-2022 papers have different DSC codes and should not be mixed for pattern.
