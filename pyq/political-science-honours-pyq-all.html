About Political Science Honours — All Semesters PYQ — Delhi University

Political Science Honours at DU for All Semesters blends theory and Indian institutions. This All Semesters PYQ with 86 document(s) orders concept and application together. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Theory — liberty, justice, democracy, Rawls/Nozick
  • Unit 2: Indian constitution — federalism, rights, institutions
  • Unit 3: Comparative & global politics — case studies
  • Unit 4: Long answers — thesis + counter-thesis + Indian example

Exam pattern & marking: 90/100 marks with choices “Answer 4 out of 6”. Theory asks comparison, India papers ask application with recent examples. Thesis + counter-thesis + one Supreme Court case or election data fetch full marks.

FAQs

How is this PYQ marked at DU?

Typically 75 marks written + 25 internal or 90+10 depending on college. Shorts are 10 marks, longs are 15. Steps, working notes and one labelled diagram or correct quote/data point per answer usually cross 7/10.

Which units repeat most in this subject?

Middle units (2-3) — theory plus application — repeat about 50% of the time in 2023-2025 PYQs. Map each past question to Units 1-4; the 10-minute PYQ-mapping exercise tells you where to spend the next two days.
