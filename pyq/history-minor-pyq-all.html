About History Minor — All Semesters PYQ — Delhi University

History Minor at DU for All Semesters spans Ancient to Contemporary. This All Semesters PYQ page with 26 document(s) groups syllabus and PYQs chronologically. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Sources & historiography — epigraphy, archaeology, literary
  • Unit 2: Polity & economy — e.g., Mauryan state, Sultanate, 1857
  • Unit 3: Society & culture — Bhakti, gender, caste
  • Unit 4: Map & long essays — locate 4 sites + historiographical debate

Exam pattern & marking: Typically 75 written + 25 internal. Map-based (10 marks), two 15-mark essays, three 10-mark shorts. Answers without source discussion rarely cross 6/10. Chronology, one primary source per answer, and concluding “why this debate matters” line are checked.
