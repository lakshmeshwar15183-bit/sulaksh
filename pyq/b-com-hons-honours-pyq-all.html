About B.Com (Hons) Honours — All Semesters PYQ — Delhi University

B.Com (Hons) Honours at DU for All Semesters follows numerical-then-theory progression. This All Semesters PYQ with 154 document(s) mirrors that order. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Accounting/Business Maths — journal, ledger, ratios
  • Unit 2: Laws & theory — Contract Act, provisions
  • Unit 3: Numericals — Process Costing, Marginal vs Absorption
  • Unit 4: Cases — GST input credit, audit, management

Exam pattern & marking: Typically 75+25 or 90+10. Sems 1-4 are 50% step-based numericals. Section A compulsory, Section B attempt 4/6. Show working notes — even wrong final answer with correct steps gets 8/12. In theory, use definition + provision + example.
