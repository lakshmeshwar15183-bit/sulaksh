About Economics Honours — Semester 3 — Delhi University

Economics Honours at DU for Semester 3 is mathematical and diagram-driven. This Semester 3 with 25 document(s) separates quantitative core first. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Micro/Macro foundations — consumer equilibrium, IS-LM
  • Unit 2: Numericals — elasticity, national income, multiplier
  • Unit 3: Theory & diagrams — AD-AS, Phillips curve
  • Unit 4: Data & policy — GDP/GNP, reforms
UnitTypical marks (62 total)Focus
Unit 1 — Foundations15Definition + 10m short
Unit 2 — Applied theory1815m debate + example
Unit 3 — Contemporary17Case / quote 10m
Unit 4 — Synthesis12Essay 15m + conclusion

Exam pattern & marking: 75 marks theory with 40% numericals. Expect one 15-mark derivation, two 10-mark numericals, and shorts. Without diagrams, scores plateau. Steps, formula, substitution, and labelled diagrams carry weight. One correct diagram often equals 8/10.

Solved example — Q1 outline (pattern from this paper)

FAQs

Do these notes cover the whole syllabus?

They cover Units 1-4 in DU’s taught order with headings, sub-points and one example or table per Unit — the format that maps directly to the marking scheme. Pair with 2-3 PYQs and one standard textbook per paper for full coverage.

How should I revise these notes in the last week?

One page per Unit — 4-5 bullets + one diagram/table + one PYQ pointer where this Unit was asked. Time-box 2 days per Unit. Verified notes on Sulaksh are formatted exactly that way.

Are these enough without the textbook?

Use the notes for structure and the textbook for depth. The notes compress 200 pages into 20 revision pages; the textbook gives the full quote or derivation when you need it.
