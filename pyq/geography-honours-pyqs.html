Study Guide — Geography Honours (DU)

Geography Honours is field- and map-driven. Examiners reward labelled diagrams, map work, and case studies with data. A well-labelled geomorphology or climate diagram often earns 8/10.

Exam pattern & marking

8-semester programme under UGCF 2022. Early semesters cover Physical Geography (Geomorphology, Climatology, Oceanography, Biogeography) and Human Geography. Later semesters add Indian Geography, Regional Planning, and Geospatial Techniques. Papers are typically 75 + 25 or 90 + 10.

High-yield units (from PYQ mapping)

  • Geomorphology — landforms, plate tectonics, cycle of erosion.
  • Climatology — atmosphere, Koppen classification, monsoon.
  • Human Geography — population, settlement, urbanisation.
  • Indian Geography — physiography, climate, resources, agriculture.
  • Geospatial Techniques — GIS, remote sensing, cartography.

Common mistakes that cost marks

  • No maps or diagrams.
  • Vague answers without data or case studies.
  • Confusing physical and human geography concepts.
  • Ignoring the map question (free marks).

30-day plan

  1. Days 1–5: Build a map sheet — 4 sites per Unit with one-line notes.
  2. Days 6–10: Make a diagram sheet (landforms, climate graphs, population pyramids).
  3. Days 11–15: Solve PYQs under timed conditions; note repeating Units.
  4. Days 16–20: Write 15-mark answers with map + data + conclusion.
  5. Days 21–25: Revise Indian Geography facts (rivers, soils, crops).
  6. Days 26–30: Full mock papers; fix time management.
