About This Collection — Reading Indian Fiction In English Generic Elective

You are expected to read primary texts — novels, plays, poems, essays — and answer with form-aware analysis. This collection with 14 documents across 1 semesters organises syllabus, PYQs and notes semester-wise so you see the reading order DU actually teaches.

Exam pattern: Most papers are 90 marks theory + 10 internal, or 75+25 depending on college.
