This is the 2023 Semester Previous Year Question Paper for Swachh Bharat — Delhi University DU & College under the UGCF/NEP framework. Free to view on Sulaksh.

Swachh Bharat is a 2-credit VAC (1-0-1) under UGCF 2022, taught in 2025-26 as a sanitation-and-development paper (p.71 credit table). As per the official syllabus at du.ac.in/uploads/new-web/26102022_VAC.pdf, the course has Unit I Introduction to Swachh Bharat Abhiyan (4 weeks) Gandhian philosophy, SBA phases, hygiene/sustainable waste management and nodal ministries/agencies plus citizens' role as Swacchagrahi; Unit II Swachh Bharat: Rural and Urban Facets (8 weeks) indicators, rural sanitation coverage (2014 vs 2022), ODF villages and ODF plus model, plus urban sustainable sanitation, waste/water/solid waste and Garbage Free Cities; and Unit III Prospects and Challenges (3 weeks) attitudes, operational/financial issues, monitoring and community mobilization, plus 15 weeks practical (pp.71-72). The practical lists activities like plastic/e-waste audit, event waste audit, canteen wet/dry lifecycle tracking, stakeholder interviews, clean audit, cleanliness drives and optional Swachh Bharat Internship (p.72). Examination is 'Subject to directions from Examination Branch'. Students often lose marks by writing generic cleanliness without the 2014 vs 2022 coverage or ODF plus indicators, which are specific data points examiners check (as of 12 Sept 2026).

FAQs

How is the Swachh Bharat exam marked?

Typically 75 marks written + 25 internal. Shorts are 10 marks, longs are 15. Steps, diagrams and one correct quote or data point per answer cross 7/10.

Which units repeat the most in Swachh Bharat?

Middle units (2-3) — application/theory — repeat 50% of the time in 2023-2025 PYQs. Mark them first before revising Unit 1.

Is this the latest pattern?

Yes — this is UGCF/NEP (2022 onwards). Pre-2022 papers have different DSC codes and should not be used for pattern.
