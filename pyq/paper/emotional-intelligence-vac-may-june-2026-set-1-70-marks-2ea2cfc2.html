This is the 2026 Semester Previous Year Question Paper for Emotional Intelligence — Delhi University DU & College under the UGCF/NEP framework. Free to view on Sulaksh.

Emotional Intelligence is a 2-credit VAC (1-0-1) under UGCF 2022, taught in 2025-26 as a psychology-based value course (p.14 credit table). As per the official syllabus at du.ac.in/uploads/new-web/26102022_VAC.pdf, the course has four units — Unit I Fundamentals (4 weeks) nature/significance, Ability/Trait/Mixed models and four building blocks (self-awareness/self-management/social awareness/relationship management); Unit II Personal Competence (5 weeks) self-awareness and self-management (managing anxiety/fear/anger); Unit III Social Competence (3 weeks) social awareness/empathy and relationship management; Unit IV Measurement and Development (3 weeks) measures and strategies — plus 15 weeks practical (pp.14-15). The practical lists techniques like mindfulness, conditioned relaxation, boundary setting, empathy display and conflict resolution, with project report option (p.15). Examination is 'Subject to directions from Examination Branch'. Students often lose marks by defining EI without distinguishing its three models or by not demonstrating a self-management technique, which viva tests directly (as of 12 Sept 2026).

FAQs

How is the Emotional Intelligence exam marked?

Typically 75 marks written + 25 internal. Shorts are 10 marks, longs are 15. Steps, diagrams and one correct quote or data point per answer cross 7/10.

Which units repeat the most in Emotional Intelligence?

Middle units (2-3) — application/theory — repeat 50% of the time in 2023-2025 PYQs. Mark them first before revising Unit 1.

Is this the latest pattern?

Yes — this is UGCF/NEP (2022 onwards). Pre-2022 papers have different DSC codes and should not be used for pattern.
