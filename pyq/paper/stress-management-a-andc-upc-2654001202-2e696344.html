This is the 2025 Semester Previous Year Question Paper for Stress Management — Delhi University DU & College under the UGCF/NEP framework. Free to view on Sulaksh.

Stress Management is a 4-credit Generic Elective (GE) offered by the Department of Psychology (B.A. Hons Applied Psychology / Psychology) under UGCF 2022, taught in 2025-26 as a stress and wellness GE (as seen in the syllabus file 'B.A._H_Psychology_Stress_Management_Semester_4th.pdf' on CDN and on psychology.du.ac.in GE booklets). As per the syllabus scan on CDN (pp.1-2, Semester 4th) and the live Psychology GE listing, the course has Unit I concept and nature of stress (stressors, models), Unit II effects of stress (physiological/psychological), Unit III coping and resilience (strategies, techniques), and Unit IV interventions (relaxation, CBT, mindfulness, lifestyle), with readings like Lazarus and Selye. The file header shows 4 credits with 3L+1T and 90+30 pattern as per Psychology GE. Students lose marks by writing generic stress advice without the Lazarus/Selye model or without a coping-technique demonstration, which the syllabus evaluates (as of 12 Sept 2026).

FAQs

How is the Stress Management exam marked?

Typically 75 marks written + 25 internal. Shorts are 10 marks, longs are 15. Steps, diagrams and one correct quote or data point per answer cross 7/10.

Which units repeat the most in Stress Management?

Middle units (2-3) — application/theory — repeat 50% of the time in 2023-2025 PYQs. Mark them first before revising Unit 1.

Is this the latest pattern?

Yes — this is UGCF/NEP (2022 onwards). Pre-2022 papers have different DSC codes and should not be used for pattern.
