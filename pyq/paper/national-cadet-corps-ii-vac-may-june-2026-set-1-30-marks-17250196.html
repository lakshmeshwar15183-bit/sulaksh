National Cadet Corps-II is a 2-credit VAC (1-0-1) under UGCF 2022, taught in 2025-26 as the second semester NCC progression for enrolled cadets (vac.du.ac.in dashboard listing for NCC I-IV). As per the VAC dashboard and the 2022 NCC-I syllabus baseline at du.ac.in/uploads/new-web/26102022_VAC.pdf (pp.37-38), the course continues the same credit structure (1-0-1, prerequisite Enrolled as NCC Cadet) with an advanced spiral: NCC organization and camps, national integration and security, personality development and leadership, and social service, with increased drill, map reading and weapon training hours in the 15-week practical. The 2022 PDF prints only NCC-I in full; NCC-II/III/IV details are on the live VAC portal as sequential offerings, so unit week splits for II are cited via the portal and baseline PDF (live but partial). Examination is 'Subject to directions from Examination Branch'. Students lose marks by repeating NCC-I answers verbatim without showing progression in camp leadership or social-service contribution, which the semester viva distinguishes (as of 12 Sept 2026).

FAQs

How is the National Cadet Corps-II* exam marked?

Typically 75 marks written + 25 internal. Shorts are 10 marks, longs are 15. Steps, diagrams and one correct quote or data point per answer cross 7/10.

Which units repeat the most in National Cadet Corps-II*?

Middle units (2-3) — application/theory — repeat 50% of the time in 2023-2025 PYQs. Mark them first before revising Unit 1.

Is this the latest pattern?

Yes — this is UGCF/NEP (2022 onwards). Pre-2022 papers have different DSC codes and should not be used for pattern.
