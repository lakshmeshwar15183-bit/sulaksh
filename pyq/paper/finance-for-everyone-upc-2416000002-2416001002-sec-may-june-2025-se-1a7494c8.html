Finance for Everyone is a 2-credit Skill Enhancement Course (SEC) under Delhi University's UGCF 2022, taught in 2025-26 as a 1 lecture, 0 tutorial, 1 practical (2 credits) (p.1 credit table). As per the official syllabus on academicaffairs.du.ac.in, the course is organised as five 3-week units — Unit 1 Financial Planning and Budgeting, Unit 2 Banking Services, Unit 3 India Post Office Financial Services, Unit 4 Insurance Services, and Unit 5 Stock Markets basic concepts. Units cover budgeting, banking, post office savings, insurance and stock market basics with practical financial planning tasks (p.1-2). Total marks 100: 25 Internal + 25 Practical + 50 End Semester (p.3). Students often memorise definitions of banking/insurance without practising a household budget, post office scheme comparison, or basic stock concept — the practical asks for those applications. For revision, map each PYQ to its unit — middle units repeat most — and confirm your college's exact paper code before the exam, as SEC codes vary by theme (as of 12 Sept 2026).
