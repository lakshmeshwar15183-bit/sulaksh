Study Guide — Hindi Honours (DU)

Hindi Honours balances kavya, katha, natak, and bhasha-vigyan across 8 semesters. Examiners reward accurate textual reference, understanding of form (chhayavaad, pragativad, nayi kavita), and clean Hindi expression.

Exam pattern & marking

Sem 1 DSCs: Hindi Kavita (Aadikal & Nirgun Bhaktikavya), Hindi Sahitya ka Itihas (Aadikal & Madhyakal), Hindi Kahani (each 4 credits). Papers are typically 75 + 25 or 90 + 10, with 10-mark shorts and 15-mark longs.

High-yield units (from PYQ mapping)

  • Kavita — Aadikal, Bhakti, Riti, Adhunik, Chhayavaad.
  • Kahani — Premchand, Agyeya, Mohan Rakesh, contemporary.
  • Natak — Bharatendu, Jayshankar Prasad, Mohan Rakesh.
  • Sahitya ka Itihas — Aadikal to Aadhunik periods.
  • Bhasha Vigyan — vyakaran, rachna, anuvad.

Common mistakes that cost marks

  • Memorising without understanding the text.
  • Weak examples from the prescribed texts.
  • Poor Hindi expression (grammar, gender, tense).
  • Ignoring the bhasha-vigyan section.

30-day plan

  1. Days 1–5: Read prescribed texts; make a one-page sheet per text (theme / form / key lines).
  2. Days 6–10: Solve 2 past papers per semester under timed conditions.
  3. Days 11–15: Map PYQs to texts; note repeating questions.
  4. Days 16–20: Write 15-mark answers with quote + analysis + conclusion.
  5. Days 21–25: Revise bhasha-vigyan rules and examples.
  6. Days 26–30: Full mock papers; fix time management.
