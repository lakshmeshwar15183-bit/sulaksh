About English As Minor — Semester 5 Syllabus — Delhi University

English As Minor at DU (NEP/UGCF) for Semester 5 is built around close reading of primary texts. This page Semester 5 Syllabus with 1 document(s) organises semester 5 syllabus in DU's taught order. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Primary texts — e.g., Chaucer, Donne, Shakespeare (form-aware reading)
  • Unit 2: Literary history & close reading — passage-based analysis
  • Unit 3: Theory — e.g., Post-colonial, Feminist, Cultural studies
  • Unit 4: Essay & long answers — thesis + textual reference

Exam pattern & marking: Most English papers are 90 theory + 10 internal or 75+25. Questions are 10 marks (short) and 15 marks (long), with passage-based questions where 1-2 quoted lines matter. Examiners reward accurate textual reference, a clear thesis in first 3 lines, and awareness of form. One-page-per-text sheet with “what's said / how it's said / one quote” maps to rubric.

Solved example — Q1 outline (pattern from this paper)

Representative Q (15m, Unit 2/3): “English As Minor — Semester 5 Syllabus — discuss with reference to Unit 2/3 concepts and one example/diagram. Critically examine.”

Outline that fetches 13/15: 1) Define in 2 lines + one authoritative quote/case/formula for English; 2) 3 points — point + example/diagram + data; 3) Counter-point (one limitation / alternative view); 4) Concluding line “so what?” linking to exam. Timebox 22 min.

Why this works for English Semester 5: Examiners check definition + evidence + conclusion. One diagram/table per answer is the fastest 3-mark gain. PYQ mapping for English shows Unit 2/3 repeats ~50% — this outline is built for that.
