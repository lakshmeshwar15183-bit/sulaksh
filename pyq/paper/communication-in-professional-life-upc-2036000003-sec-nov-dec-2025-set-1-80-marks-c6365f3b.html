Communication in Professional Life is a 2-credit Skill Enhancement Course (SEC) under Delhi University's UGCF 2022, taught in 2025-26 as a 0 lecture, 0 tutorial, 2 practical (p.1 credit table). As per the official syllabus on academicaffairs.du.ac.in, the course is organised as four blocks — Unit 1 Theory of Business Communication (8 hours), Unit 2 Listening Skills (14 hours), Unit 3 Speaking Skills including presentation, group discussion, TED/business talks, persuasion, negotiation and PR functions (18 hours), and Unit 4 Writing Skills including CV/resume, documentation, reports, social media posts/blog/review and Canva poster/profile making (20 hours). Unit 4 builds job-oriented skills: CV, bio-data, application letter, advertisements/invitation, analytical/project reports, and LinkedIn profile (p.2). Evaluation is 'as per guidelines notified by the University of Delhi' (p.3). Common loss is submitting a plain paragraph for a resume or report instead of the format examiners expect — proper CV sections, business letter layout, or Canva poster — and skipping the practical viva on persuasion and meeting etiquette. For revision, map each PYQ to its unit — middle units repeat most — and confirm your college's exact paper code before the exam, as SEC codes vary by theme (as of 12 Sept 2026).

FAQs

How is the Communication in Professional Life exam marked?

Typically 75 marks written + 25 internal. Shorts are 10 marks, longs are 15. Steps, diagrams and one correct quote or data point per answer cross 7/10.

Which units repeat the most in Communication in Professional Life?

Middle units (2-3) — application/theory — repeat 50% of the time in 2023-2025 PYQs. Mark them first before revising Unit 1.

Is this the latest pattern?

Yes — this is UGCF/NEP (2022 onwards). Pre-2022 papers have different DSC codes and should not be used for pattern.
