B.Sc. (Hons) Geology at Delhi University moves from earth systems and minerals to structural, igneous, palaeontological and applied geology. As of 2026, the UGCF 2022 structure lists Sem 1 DSCs as Earth System Science (UPC 2192011101), Mineral Science (2192011102) and Concepts of Stratigraphy (2192011103), each 4 credits (3 theory + 1 practical). Later semesters cover Structural Geology, Igneous Petrology, Palaeontology, Sedimentary and Metamorphic Geology, Hydrogeology and Economic Geology. Students often lose marks on field-based papers — Geological Mapping (2T+2P in Sem 5) and Remote Sensing & GIS — where map work, cross-sections and neat field records are assessed strictly.
