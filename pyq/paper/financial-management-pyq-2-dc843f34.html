B.Com Programme at Delhi University covers accounting, law, management and economics as a balanced commerce degree rather than a specialised honours. As of 2026, the UGCF 2022 programme lists Sem 1 DSCs as Business organisation and management (UPC 2412091101), Business Laws (2412091102) and Financial Accounting (2412091103, 3T+1P), each 4 credits. Sem 2-4 then add Corporate Accounting, Company Law, Business Statistics, Financial Management and Entrepreneurship. Students often lose marks on numerical papers — Financial and Cost Accounting, Business Statistics — where format, ledger balancing and formula application are scored step-wise.
