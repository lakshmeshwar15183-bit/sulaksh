History Honours at Delhi University under UGCF 2022 is a chronological and thematic programme across 8 semesters, moving from Ancient India to Contemporary World. Early semesters cover Ancient Societies, History of India I (up to 300 CE) and World History, while later semesters include Medieval India (Sultanate, Mughal India with administration, Mansabdari, Rajput and Maratha relations), Modern India (1857, nationalism, Partition) and contemporary India and world history with historiography. Each DSC is 4 credits (3L+1T) with Units 1-4 spanning sources, polity, economy, society and culture, plus map-based questions where you locate and explain 4 sites. Examiners reward source discussion (epigraphy vs literary), clear chronology and one primary source per answer, with a concluding historiographical note. Students often lose marks writing generic narratives without period brackets or without the specific monument, edict or traveller account from the reading list — for Mughal India, for example, anchoring Mansabdari evolution from Akbar to Aurangzeb and linking Taj Mahal/Red Fort patronage to state structure is essential.
