B.A. (Hons) Sanskrit at Delhi University under UGCF 2022 is a classical-language programme across 8 semesters, moving from Sanskrit poetry and prose to grammar, poetics, philosophy and Indian knowledge systems. Early semesters cover Classical Sanskrit Literature (Kavya) — e.g., Meghadutam, Abhijnanashakuntalam selections, Niti literature — and Critical Survey of Sanskrit Literature with Vyakarana basics (Ashtadhyayi, Siddhanta Kaumudi selections). Middle semesters add Sanskrit Grammar (Laghu Siddhanta Kaumudi — Samjna, Sandhi, Karaka), Classical Literature (Poetry, Drama, Prose), Darshana — Indian Philosophy (Nyaya, Vedanta, Sankhya, Yoga readings), and Poetics & Aesthetics (Kavya Shastra — Rasa, Alankara, Dhvani). Later semesters cover Modern Sanskrit Literature, Vedic Literature and Sanskrit linguistics. Each DSC is 4 credits (3L+1T) with Units 1-4 spanning textual reading, grammar analysis, literary history and philosophical interpretation. Examiners reward accurate verse translation with anvaya and word-analysis, correct sandhi/samasa vigraha, and alankara/rasa identification with textual citation, plus a concluding cultural note. Students often lose marks by writing Hindi translation without Sanskrit sutra reference, by ignoring chhanda or alankara labels, and by quoting shlokas without metre or source — precise textual citation with grammatical justification is essential.
