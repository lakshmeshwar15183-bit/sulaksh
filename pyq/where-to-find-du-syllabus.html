Where to Find the DU Syllabus (Semester-wise)?

Your syllabus is the roadmap - every exam question comes strictly from it. Here's where the official versions live:

1. Official DU Website

du.ac.in hosts complete UGCF/NEP syllabi under Academic Affairs - unit-wise content, credit distribution, readings and exam pattern for every department and semester.

2. Sulaksh - Subject-wise Syllabus Collection

Sulaksh maintains syllabus documents organised by subject and programme - CORE subjects (English, History, Economics, PolSci, Maths, Hindi, B.Com), plus GE/VAC/AEC/SEC courses. All free, all semester-tagged.

3. Your College Department

Your department shares the applicable syllabus each semester - cross-check it against the university version to confirm your exact paper codes.

Why This Matters More Than You Think

DUs NEP exams test strictly within syllabus boundaries. Students who master the listed units consistently outperform those who study "everything". Know your units, their weightage and prioritise accordingly.
