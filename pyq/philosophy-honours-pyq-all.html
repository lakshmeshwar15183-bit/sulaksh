About Philosophy Honours — All Semesters PYQ — Delhi University

Philosophy Honours at DU under NEP/UGCF for All Semesters focuses on conceptual clarity. This All Semesters PYQ with 125 document(s) groups syllabus, notes and PYQs in taught order. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Foundations & concepts — core readings
  • Unit 2: Applied theory — case studies and examples
  • Unit 3: Contemporary issues — debates and perspectives
  • Unit 4: Practice & long answers — thesis + evidence

FAQs

How is this PYQ marked at DU?

Typically 75 marks written + 25 internal or 90+10 depending on college. Shorts are 10 marks, longs are 15. Steps, working notes and one labelled diagram or correct quote/data point per answer usually cross 7/10.

Which units repeat most in this subject?

Middle units (2-3) — theory plus application — repeat about 50% of the time in 2023-2025 PYQs. Map each past question to Units 1-4; the 10-minute PYQ-mapping exercise tells you where to spend the next two days.

Is this the latest UGCF/NEP pattern?

Yes — papers from 2023-2026 follow the current UGCF/NEP framework. Pre-2022 papers have different DSC codes and should not be mixed for pattern.
