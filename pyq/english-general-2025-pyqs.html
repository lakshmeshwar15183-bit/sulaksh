About English General — Papers 2025 — Delhi University

English General at DU (NEP/UGCF) for Papers is built around close reading of primary texts. This page Papers 2025 with 57 document(s) organises papers material in DU's taught order.

Units (verify from your college syllabus handout):

  • Unit 1: Primary texts — e.g., Chaucer, Donne, Shakespeare (form-aware reading)
  • Unit 2: Literary history & close reading — passage-based analysis
  • Unit 3: Theory — e.g., Post-colonial, Feminist, Cultural studies
  • Unit 4: Essay & long answers — thesis + textual reference

Official sources: University of Delhi · DU Exam Portal · your college's English syllabus handout.
