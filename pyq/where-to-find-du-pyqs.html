Where to Find DU Previous Year Question Papers (Free)?

Every DU student knows - previous year question papers are 50% of your preparation. DU repeats concepts, question styles and sometimes full questions across cycles. The struggle is finding them in one place. Here's where to look:

1. Sulaksh.online - One-Click Collection (Recommended)

The fastest way: Sulaksh's complete PYQ collection. Hundreds of DU papers across BA Programme majors/minors, B.Com and skill courses - organised semester-wise and track-wise (Honours/Major/Minor). Every paper opens instantly, no sign-up, no payment.

2. Your College Library / Department Office

Most colleges keep archives of past sem papers. Ask your CR or department office - internal tests often circulate there first.

3. Official DU Portals

du.ac.in publishes date sheets, syllabi and notifications. Previous papers themselves aren't centrally published - which is exactly why college collections and Sulaksh fill the gap.

Bonus Tip

Solve at least 3-5 recent years per paper, time yourself like the real exam, and mark questions that repeat across sets - those are your highest-probability exam questions.
