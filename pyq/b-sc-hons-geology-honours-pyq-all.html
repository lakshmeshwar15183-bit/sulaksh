About B.Sc. (Hons) Geology Honours — All Semesters PYQ — Delhi University

B.Sc. (Hons) Geology Honours at DU for All Semesters combines theory, practicals and labs. This All Semesters PYQ with 73 document(s) keeps lab and theory aligned. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Theory & derivation — e.g., thermodynamics, cell structure
  • Unit 2: Mechanisms/diagrams — organic, genetics, circuits
  • Unit 3: Practical & viva — lab steps and observations
  • Unit 4: Short notes & applications — one diagram per answer

Exam pattern & marking: 75 theory + 25 internal, plus 25-50 practical. One derivation/diagram (15 marks), two mechanisms (10 each), and shorts. Labelled diagrams with equations carry disproportionate weight.
