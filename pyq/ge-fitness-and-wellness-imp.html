About Fitness and Wellness — Generic Elective at Delhi University

Fitness and Wellness is a 4-credit Generic Elective (GE) under Delhi University's UGCF/NEP 2022. It is offered to students from other disciplines as an interdisciplinary elective, usually in Semester 1 to 6. Health and fitness GE. This page brings together syllabus, previous year papers and study material so you can see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college): The DU syllabus is organised into four units. For Fitness and Wellness, the broad coverage is:

  • Unit 1: Fitness, exercise & nutrition
  • Unit 2: Yoga & lifestyle management
  • Unit 3: Sports & injury prevention
  • Unit 4: Wellness plan & assessment

Exam pattern & marking: Typically 4 credits, 75 marks theory + 25 internal/tutorial or 90+10 depending on college. Sections with choices — e.g., answer 4 out of 6. Units 2-3 carry the most weight, with diagram or data questions where applicable. Internal assessment includes tutorials and presentations. Examiners look for unit-wise structure, one relevant example per answer, and a clear conclusion.
