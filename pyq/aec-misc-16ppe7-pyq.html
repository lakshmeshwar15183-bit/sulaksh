About हिंदी भाषा : सम्प्रेषण और संचार (क) — Delhi University

हिंदी भाषा : सम्प्रेषण और संचार (Hindi Ka) is a 2-credit AEC (2-0-0, lecture 2) for students who studied Hindi till Class 12th, taught in 2025-26 as AEC-1 (p.77 credit table, du.ac.in/uploads/new-web/24022023_AEC.pdf). As per the official syllabus at that live PDF, the course has two blocks — इकाई 1 सम्प्रेषण: सामान्य परिचय (1-7 weeks) सम्प्रेषण की अवधारणा/प्रक्रिया/विविध आयाम and सम्प्रेषण और संचार; and इकाई 2 सम्प्रेषण और संचार के विविध रूप (8-15 weeks) सम्प्रेषण के प्रकार, COVID/mansik swasthya etc. par sarvekshan-adharit report, anuched/samvad/diary and blog/sampadakiya lekhan, plus sahayak granth list including Sudhish Pachauri and Smita Mishra (pp.77-78). The assessment printed is कुल अंक 50: लिखित परीक्षा 38 अंक लिखित, आंतरिक 12 अंक (p.78). Students often lose marks by summarising Sampreshan definitions without the sarvekshan-report or blog/sampadakiya samples the syllabus specifies, which the 38-mark written paper asks (as of 12 Sept 2026).

FAQs

How is this PYQ marked at DU?

Typically 75 marks written + 25 internal or 90+10 depending on college. Shorts are 10 marks, longs are 15. Steps, working notes and one labelled diagram or correct quote/data point per answer usually cross 7/10.

Which units repeat most in this subject?

Middle units (2-3) — theory plus application — repeat about 50% of the time in 2023-2025 PYQs. Map each past question to Units 1-4; the 10-minute PYQ-mapping exercise tells you where to spend the next two days.

Is this the latest UGCF/NEP pattern?

Yes — papers from 2023-2026 follow the current UGCF/NEP framework. Pre-2022 papers have different DSC codes and should not be mixed for pattern.
