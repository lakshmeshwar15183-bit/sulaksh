Advanced Spreadsheets Tools is a 2-credit SEC (0 lecture +0 tutorial +2 practical, Theme 17 Sl.88) under UGCF 2022, offered in Semesters 3–5 and verified for 2025-26 as a fully practical paper. The official PDF organises it in four timed blocks: Unit 1 Excel Advanced Techniques (3 weeks) — templates, Standard Deviation/Variance/Coefficient of Variation, Data Validation, lookup/power/array formulae, advanced range names, Goal Seek/Data Tables/Scenario Manager and the Data Analysis ToolPak with Solver and regression for projection; Unit 2 Excel Interactivity and Automation (4 weeks) — Index/Match, Offset, dynamic charting, database/text/error functions, form controls and basic VBA with absolute/relative macros, loops and UserForms. Units 3 and 4 complete it: Unit 3 Introduction to VBA (4 weeks) on conditional formatting, charts that inspire (Waterfall, Thermometer, Histogram), Slicers, Sparklines and graphics tricks, and Unit 4 Data Analysis and Decision-Making (4 weeks) on external data, advanced PivotTables, PowerPivot/Power Query, dashboards and applied sheets for loan/lease, ratio/payroll, NPV/IRR, breakeven and optimisation. A DU-specific verified detail is the printed marking scheme on page 3: Total 100 — Internal Assessment 25, Practical Exam (Internal) 25, End Semester University Exam 50 — with Internal Assessment left to faculty. Students lose marks because they revise by reading PDFs; the paper is marked on file work and viva, and a workbook without Ctrl+~ formula views, without a working Goal Seek or Solver model, or without a PivotTable-driven dashboard scores low even if definitions are memorised as taught in 2025-26.

About Advanced Spreadsheets Tools — SEC at Delhi University

Advanced Spreadsheets Tools is a 2-credit Skill Enhancement Course (SEC) under Delhi University's UGCF/NEP 2022. It is offered across BA, BSc and BCom programmes, usually in Semester 3 to 5 depending on your college. Mastering Excel/Google Sheets for business and research. This page brings together syllabus, previous year papers and study material in one place so you can see the taught order and the exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college): The DU syllabus is organised into four units. For Advanced Spreadsheets Tools, the broad coverage is:

  • Unit 1: Advanced formulas, lookup & logic functions
  • Unit 2: PivotTables, PivotCharts & data modelling
  • Unit 3: Macros, VBA basics & automation
  • Unit 4: Dashboard design, what-if analysis & data validation

Exam pattern & marking: The paper is typically theory plus practical/internal assessment — often 50-75 marks theory with 25 marks internal/practical, or 2-credit evaluation via continuous assessment, presentations and a semester-end exam. Questions are a mix of short notes (5-6 marks) and applied long answers (10-15 marks) drawn directly from the four units. Examiners look for unit-wise structure, one relevant example or tool per answer, and a clear conclusion. For practical SECs, viva and file work carry significant weight.
