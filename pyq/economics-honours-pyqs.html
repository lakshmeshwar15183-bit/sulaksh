About This Collection — Economics Honours

Economics Honours at DU is mathematical and diagram-driven. This collection with 180 documents across 8 semester(s) separates Micro, Macro, Statistics and Maths so you revise the quantitative core first.

Exam pattern: 75 marks theory with numericals (40% weight) and theory (60%). Expect one 15-mark derivation (e.g., IS-LM, consumer equilibrium), two 10-mark numericals (elasticity, national income), and shorts on definitions. Without diagrams, scores plateau at 45/75.

Marking: Steps matter — writing the formula, substituting, and final unit (Rs., %). In theory, a labelled diagram (Indifference curves, AD-AS) with a 2-line explanation often gets 8/10 even with brief text.

How to use PYQs: For each unit, solve last 3 years’ numericals first, then theory. You will see 50% repeat — e.g., GDP vs GNP, multiplier, Phillips curve debate. Keep a formula sheet per semester; verified notes here do the same.

All papers here are UGCF/NEP, free to view. Cross-check the official syllabus for exact numerical weightage for your batch.

Official links: University of Delhi · DU Exam Portal

FAQs

Do these notes cover the whole syllabus?

They cover Units 1-4 in DU’s taught order with headings, sub-points and one example or table per Unit — the format that maps directly to the marking scheme. Pair with 2-3 PYQs and one standard textbook per paper for full coverage.

How should I revise these notes in the last week?

One page per Unit — 4-5 bullets + one diagram/table + one PYQ pointer where this Unit was asked. Time-box 2 days per Unit. Verified notes on Sulaksh are formatted exactly that way.

Are these enough without the textbook?

Use the notes for structure and the textbook for depth. The notes compress 200 pages into 20 revision pages; the textbook gives the full quote or derivation when you need it.
