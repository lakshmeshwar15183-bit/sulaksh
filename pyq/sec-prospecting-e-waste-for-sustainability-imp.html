About Prospecting E-Waste for Sustainability — SEC at Delhi University

Offered as a Skill Enhancement Course (SEC) under DU's UGCF/NEP, Prospecting E-Waste for Sustainability carries 2 credits and is open to students from all streams. Colleges typically slot it in Semesters 3-5. E-waste management for sustainability. This page brings together syllabus, previous year papers and study material in one place so you can see the taught order and the exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college): The DU syllabus is organised into four units. For Prospecting E-Waste for Sustainability, the broad coverage is:

  • Unit 1: E-waste types, sources & hazards
  • Unit 2: Collection, dismantling & recycling
  • Unit 3: Policy, EPR & circular economy
  • Unit 4: Sustainable enterprise models

Exam pattern & marking: The paper is typically theory plus practical/internal assessment — often 50-75 marks theory with 25 marks internal/practical, or 2-credit evaluation via continuous assessment, presentations and a semester-end exam. Questions are a mix of short notes (5-6 marks) and applied long answers (10-15 marks) drawn directly from the four units. Examiners look for unit-wise structure, one relevant example or tool per answer, and a clear conclusion. For practical SECs, viva and file work carry significant weight.
