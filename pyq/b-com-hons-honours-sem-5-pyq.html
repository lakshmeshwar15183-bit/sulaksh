About B.Com (Hons) Honours — Semester 5 PYQ — Delhi University

B.Com (Hons) Honours at DU for Semester 5 follows numerical-then-theory progression. This Semester 5 PYQ with 23 document(s) mirrors that order. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Accounting/Business Maths — journal, ledger, ratios
  • Unit 2: Laws & theory — Contract Act, provisions
  • Unit 3: Numericals — Process Costing, Marginal vs Absorption
  • Unit 4: Cases — GST input credit, audit, management
