Study Guide — Mathematics Honours (DU)

Mathematics Honours is proof- and problem-driven. Examiners reward clean logical steps, correct notation, and complete proofs. A correct final answer without working fetches 0.

Exam pattern & marking

Sem 1 DSCs: Algebra, Elementary Real Analysis, Probability & Statistics (each 4 credits). Later semesters build through Linear Algebra, Calculus, Group Theory, Riemann Integration, Multivariate Calculus, and ODEs. Most papers are 75 + 25 or 90 + 10.

High-yield units (from PYQ mapping)

  • Algebra — groups, rings, fields, homomorphisms.
  • Real Analysis — limits, continuity, differentiability, Riemann integration.
  • Linear Algebra — vector spaces, matrices, eigenvalues.
  • Probability & Statistics — distributions, expectation, sampling.
  • Calculus — multivariate, partial derivatives, Lagrange.

Common mistakes that cost marks

  • Skipping steps in proofs.
  • Wrong notation or missing quantifiers.
  • Not stating the theorem being used.
  • Arithmetic errors in numericals.

30-day plan

  1. Days 1–5: Make a theorem-proof sheet per Unit (statement + proof outline).
  2. Days 6–10: Solve 5 problems per Unit under timed conditions.
  3. Days 11–15: Revise definitions and counter-examples.
  4. Days 16–20: Write full proofs; get feedback on notation.
  5. Days 21–25: Practice high-weight Units (2–3) using PYQ mapping.
  6. Days 26–30: Full mock papers; fix time management.
