About Economics Honours — All Semesters PYQ — Delhi University

Economics Honours at DU for All Semesters is mathematical and diagram-driven. This All Semesters PYQ with 150 document(s) separates quantitative core first. This page helps you see the taught order and exam pattern together.

What you will study (broad UGCF outline — verify from your college):

  • Unit 1: Micro/Macro foundations — consumer equilibrium, IS-LM
  • Unit 2: Numericals — elasticity, national income, multiplier
  • Unit 3: Theory & diagrams — AD-AS, Phillips curve
  • Unit 4: Data & policy — GDP/GNP, reforms

Exam pattern & marking: 75 marks theory with 40% numericals. Expect one 15-mark derivation, two 10-mark numericals, and shorts. Without diagrams, scores plateau. Steps, formula, substitution, and labelled diagrams carry weight. One correct diagram often equals 8/10.
